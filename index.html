About

Owner on site. Every job.

JH Remodeling was founded by Jamil Hasanov, who came to the Bay Area from Azerbaijan and built the company one small job at a time: show up, do it right, redo anything that isn't. Twelve years on, the crew is bigger and the projects are larger, but the rule hasn't changed.

Homeowners mention the same things in review after review: Jamil answers the phone, sends video updates, treats your house like his own, and quotes a fair price for careful work. If a part is discontinued, he builds one to match. If a subfloor is rotten under the tile, he tells you and fixes it properly instead of tiling over it.
